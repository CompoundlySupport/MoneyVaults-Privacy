MoneyVaults: Finances & FIRE — User Manual

Complete reference guide for personal wealth tracking, Evolution & Trend Curves, interactive Calendar View, compound simulations, stock vaults, and dividends.

🏛️ 1. Philosophy & Architecture

MoneyVaults is built upon an offline-first architecture. Your data is your property: no cloud servers require your financial records, no third-party tracking scripts log your income, and no bank credentials are ever requested.

🗄️ 2. Vault Management, Sorting & Stock Portfolios

Vault Categories:

  • Piggy Bank: Goals with target amounts and milestone progress bars.
  • Emergency Fund: Calculates monthly burn rate and runway months covered.
  • Investment: Simulates compound growth, dividend projections, and capital goals.
  • Stock / Equity: Track ticker symbols, units, average acquisition cost (PRU), live P&L, and dedicated Buy & Sell orders.
  • General Account & Cash Flow Intelligence: Central clearinghouse for income, expenses, and inter-vault transfers. Premium unlocks Cash Flow Intelligence directly inside the General Account details with key analytics: all-time and period record expenses/incomes, top expenditure and revenue tags, period starting and ending balances with net variations (absolute and %), and daily average spending.

Vault Sorting & Drag & Drop Reordering: In the Premium version, reorder vault cards effortlessly using the 3-dots drag handle (⋮⋮). Your customized sequence is automatically remembered across app restarts, and the Custom Order option in the Sort Vaults menu lets you return to your preferred order anytime. You can also sort by Default, Balance, Name, or Vault Type. Tap Update P&L, Update Price, or Update Base to open an instant auto-selected numeric input dialog where typing immediately replaces the old figure.

Cash Flow Control ("Include in the cash flow" Switch): Every expense, vault contribution, withdrawal, stock trade, planned item, and recurring rule features a unified "Include in the cash flow" switch. Turn it off when logging reimbursed expenses, prepaid card spending, or internal portfolio transfers that shouldn't affect your liquid cash balance.

Tag Manager: Create and color-code custom tags to classify transactions and filter views effortlessly.

📈 3. Evolution & Trend Curves (Vaults & Portfolio)

Analyze historical and real-time wealth progression through the Vaults Evolution & Trends screen:

  • Multi-Horizon Evolution Curves: Inspect individual vaults or your entire portfolio across 1M (Daily), 3M (Daily), YTD (Weekly), 1Y (Weekly), and ALL (Monthly) horizons.
  • Natural Dual-Curve Scale with Live P&L % Scrubbing: Simultaneously compare Current Balance and Profit & Loss (P&L) on a natural monetary scale with a dashed 0 baseline and Y-axis reference labels. Dragging your finger across the interactive chart updates the cursor tooltip and recalculates the dynamic P&L percentage (+€XX.XX (+YY.Y%)) in real time for Investment, Stock, and Asset vaults.
  • Comprehensive Performance KPIs: Refactored KPI metrics clearly report Total P&L, Total P&L %, Period P&L, and Period P&L %. The Period Change card explicitly details the total variation separated into net capital inflows versus pure P&L market performance.
  • Automated 00:00 Snapshots & FIFO Pruning: At 00:00 local time, MoneyVaults captures daily, weekly, and monthly snapshots using First-In-First-Out (FIFO) retention pruning to keep storage ultra-compact. Periods prior to a vault's creation date automatically render at 0.
  • Monthly Stacked Bar Evolution: Compare multi-month cumulative balances and monthly net cash flows across 3-month and 6-month windows with native localized month names.
  • Quick Time Filter Reset: In any Vault Details screen, easily reset custom timeframe drilldowns back to the full history with a single tap on the Clear Time Filter button.

📅 4. Interactive Monthly Calendar View & Cash Flow Filtering

Switch seamlessly between the Transaction List View and the Interactive Monthly Calendar View on the Cash Flow screen:

  • Monthly Grid & Daily Summaries: Visualize daily inflows, outflows, and net totals at a glance.
  • Status Filter Pills: Filter the calendar by All, Completed, Planned, or Recurring items.
  • Direct Day Actions & Future Editing: Tap any date to inspect its transactions, confirm due items in 1 tap, modify a future one-time planned transaction, or edit a future recurring rule (affecting only the selected and subsequent future occurrences without creating premature completed entries).
  • Advanced Filter Bar: Combine the Exclude in Cash Flow Filter (leftmost toggle to hide transactions with "Exclude in Cash Flow" active), Title Search, Vault Filter, Tag Filter, Category Filter, and Time Range Filter (This Month, Last Month, Last 3M, Last 6M, YTD, Last Year, or Custom Date Range).

💰 5. Dividend Tracker, Projections & Stock Sync

Track and project passive income from equity holdings:

  • Distributions: Log one-time or recurring (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual) dividends.
  • Stock Link: Connect dividends to stock vaults to estimate payouts based on shares owned.
  • Tax Withholding: Configure withholding rates to project realistic net take-home cash.
  • Dividend Projections Dashboard: Switch between 1M, 3M, 6M, End of Year, and 1Y projection horizons.
  • Monthly Average Reference Line: Continuous edge-to-edge dashed reference line with a floating pill badge highlighting your average monthly dividend income.
  • Batch Management: Expand cards for localized unit breakdowns and use Select All, Deselect All, and Delete Selected for efficient management.
  • Payout Confirmations: Confirm dividends upon payment directly into Cash Flow.

🗓️ 6. Future Planned Transactions

Schedule future commitments with target dates. Planned items do not affect your current liquid balance until their due date, when the app prompts you to confirm, edit, or settle them. You can also modify any future planned transaction directly from the Calendar View.

🧮 7. Financial Calculators Suite

  • Compound Growth & FIRE: Maximized full-width trajectory charts over horizons up to 50 years, computing your FIRE retirement number, compound reinvestment advantage legend, and deposits vs interest breakdown.
  • Dividend Growth & Yield: DRIP reinvestment modeling, gross/net yield, and taxation analysis.
  • Loan Amortization: Maximized remaining principal balance trajectory chart and transparent schedule separating regular principal, extra amortizations, interest saved, and time ahead of schedule.
  • Discount Calculator (100% Free): Single and parallel discount comparisons with sales tax.

🌐 10. Multi-Language & Universal Currency

MoneyVaults natively supports 6 languages (English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German) and universal world currencies (€, $, £, R$, ¥, CHF, etc.) with automatic system matching and on-demand switching.

☁️ 11. Data Center, Cloud Backup & Biometric Security

Create 1-tap backups to Google Drive, iCloud, or local storage anytime through the Data Center. Data—including vaults, transactions, historical evolution snapshots, recurring rules, and custom tags—is exported as clean JSON files that can be restored on any device without loss. To safeguard against accidental data erasure, triggering a Backup & Factory Reset requires mandatory biometric authentication (fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN).
