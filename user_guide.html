MoneyVaults: Finances & FIRE — User Manual

Complete reference guide for personal wealth tracking, compound simulations, stock vaults, and dividends.
← Legal & Privacy Center GitHub Repository ↗

🏛️ 1. Philosophy & Architecture

MoneyVaults is built upon an offline-first architecture. Your data is your property: no cloud servers require your financial records, no third-party tracking scripts log your income, and no bank credentials are ever requested.

🗄️ 2. Vault Management & Stock Portfolios

Vault Categories:

  • Piggy Bank: Goals with target amounts and milestone progress bars.
  • Emergency Fund: Calculates monthly burn rate and runway months covered.
  • Investment: Simulates compound growth, dividend projections, and capital goals.
  • Stock / Equity: Track ticker symbols, units, average cost (PRU), live P&L, and dedicated Buy & Sell orders.
  • Expense: Fastfill-enabled accounts for seamless one-tap daily spending.

Independent Cash Flow ("Deduct from Income" Switch): Log expenses for budget tracking without subtracting from liquid cash balances (ideal for reimbursed expenses or prepaid budgets).

Tag Manager: Create and color-code custom tags to classify transactions and filter views effortlessly.

💰 3. Dividend Tracker & Stock Sync

Track passive income from equity holdings:

  • Distributions: Log one-time or recurring (monthly, quarterly, annual) dividends.
  • Stock Link: Connect dividends to stock vaults to estimate payouts based on shares owned.
  • Tax Withholding: Configure withholding rates to project realistic net take-home cash.
  • Payout Confirmations: Confirm dividends upon payment directly into Cash Flow.

📅 4. Future Planned Transactions

Schedule future commitments with target dates. Planned items do not affect your current liquid balance until their due date, when the app prompts you to confirm or settle.

📊 5. Vaults Monthly Evolution

Visualize portfolio growth with interactive multi-month stacked bar charts across 3-month and 6-month horizons, switching between Cumulative Balance and Monthly Net Flow with native localized month names.

🧮 6. Financial Calculators Suite

  • Compound Growth & FIRE: Project wealth over horizons up to 50 years, compute your FIRE retirement number, and analyze compound interest vs deposits.
  • Dividend Growth & Yield: DRIP reinvestment modeling, gross/net yield, and taxation analysis.
  • Loan Amortization: Transparent schedule separating regular principal, extra amortizations, interest, and remaining balance.
  • Discount Calculator (100% Free): Single and parallel discount comparisons with sales tax.

🔁 7. Recurring Transactions & Smart Device Alerts

Automate routine expenses, income, subscriptions, and investments:

  • Flexible Schedules: Every x Days, Weeks, Months, or Years with custom due dates (1–31) and exact notification times.
  • Device Alerts: Local push notifications with 1-tap Confirm & Log, Modify & Log, or Skip.

🎓 8. Interactive Step-by-Step Tutorials

7 guided walkthrough modal dialogs covering General Account, Investment Vaults, Recurring Rules, Planned Transactions, Stocks Vault, Dividend Tracker, and Cash Flow Independence across 6 languages.

🌐 9. Multi-Language & Universal Currency

MoneyVaults natively supports 6 languages (English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German) and universal world currencies (€, $, £, R$, ¥, CHF, etc.) with automatic system matching and on-demand switching.

☁️ 10. Data Center & Cloud Backup

You can create 1-tap backups to Google Drive, iCloud, or local storage anytime through the Data Center. Data is exported as clean, encrypted JSON files that can be restored on any device without loss.
