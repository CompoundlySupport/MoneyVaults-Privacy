🛡️ Part I: Privacy Policy

Offline-First Privacy Architecture: MoneyVaults: Finances & FIRE stores your financial records, smart vaults, evolution & trend snapshots, stock positions, dividend entries, and transaction logs locally on your device. We do not require account registration, and we do not transmit your personal financial logs to developer servers.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because MoneyVaults operates offline on your device, we do not collect or store:

  • Personal identifiers such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number.
  • Bank credentials, bank account numbers, credit card details, or banking logins.
  • Your specific budget entries, savings goals, asset trades, dividend distributions, or net worth values.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

The following data remains strictly inside your local device storage:

  • Smart Vaults & Asset Portfolios: Vault names, target amounts, categories, and dedicated stock/equity portfolios (ticker symbols, units owned, average acquisition cost PRU, market prices, and buy/sell trade logs).
  • Cash Flow Intelligence & General Account KPIs: Locally computed operational metrics for the General Account (all-time and period record expenses/incomes, top spending/revenue tags, starting/ending balances with absolute and percentage variations, and daily spending averages).
  • Evolution & Trend Curve Snapshots: Locally computed 00:00 daily, weekly, and monthly vault snapshots (for 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y, and All-Time trend curves) with automatic First-In-First-Out (FIFO) local retention pruning, real-time dynamic P&L % touch scrubbing, and period change breakdowns (separating net capital inflows from net investment P&L).
  • Cash Flow, Calendar & Planned Commitments: Everyday transactions, interactive monthly calendar items, planned one-time future transactions with scheduled due dates, and cash flow inclusion settings ("Include in the cash flow" toggle).
  • Dividend Tracker & Projections: Payout amounts, tax withholding rates, payment frequency, linked stock vaults, multi-horizon forecasts (1M, 3M, 6M, End of Year, 1Y), and monthly average reference indicators.
  • Recurring Rules & Alerts: Scheduled recurring rules, monthly due days, Day 10 trial end cancellation reminders, and local device push notification schedules.
  • Tags & Categories: User-defined custom tags, color palettes, and category mappings.
  • User Preferences & Custom Layouts: Base currency formatting, multilingual settings, persistent custom vault order (via Drag & Drop 3-dots handle), sort options (including Custom Order), and view modes (compact vs expanded, list vs calendar).
  • Temporary Pro Passes: Local timestamp tracking expiration of ad-rewarded Pro Passes (30 seconds each, up to 5 per 24-hour window). Upon pass expiration or subscription conclusion, all screens, custom filters, and display modes automatically revert to safe default settings.
  • Backups & Biometric Reset Safeguard: Manual JSON backup files (including vaults, transactions, recurring rules, dividends, and trend snapshots) generated solely when you choose to export your data. Initiating a complete Backup & Factory Reset requires local device biometric authentication (fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN) to prevent accidental data erasure.

3. Third-Party Services & Technical Data

The App integrates trusted industry-standard SDKs solely for subscriptions and optional rewarded passes:

  • In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play Billing): Processes anonymous app user IDs, transaction receipts, active subscription status, and purchase dates to unlock and restore Premium features. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Rewarded Video Ads (Google AdMob): When you voluntarily watch a video ad to unlock a 30-second Pro Pass (limited to 5 passes per 24 hours), Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers (GAID), device diagnostics, and ad interaction data to prevent fraud. See Google Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used only for optional, locally scheduled expense check-ins, weekly wealth reviews, recurring transaction due alerts, planned transaction reminders, dividend reminders, and the automated Day 10 trial end reminder notification.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Used strictly for local device authentication before executing sensitive actions such as a complete Backup & Factory Reset. Biometric data is validated solely by your device's operating system security hardware and is never accessed, processed, or stored by the App.
  • INTERNET: Required only for Google Play Billing validation and AdMob video playback.

5. Data Deletion

You have complete control: you can clear all financial data at any time through the App's Data Center (protected by biometric authentication before executing a Factory Reset) or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.
