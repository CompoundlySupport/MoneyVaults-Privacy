## 🛡️ Part I: Privacy Policy

### Core Privacy Commitment
**MoneyVaults: Finances & FIRE** is engineered from the ground up on a strict **offline-first architecture**. Your financial transactions, portfolio balances, evolution & trend snapshots, stock positions, dividend records, and savings vaults are processed and stored exclusively on your device. We do not require account registration, and we do not store, track, or transmit your personal financial logs to external servers.

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
Because MoneyVaults operates offline on your device, we do **not** collect or store on developer servers:
* Personal identifiers such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number.
* Bank credentials, bank account numbers, debit/credit card details, or Open Banking tokens.
* Your specific transaction entries, asset purchases, dividend payouts, net worth, or budget goals.

### 2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
The following data remains strictly inside your device's private local storage:
* **Vaults & Portfolio Assets:** Vault names, target goals, categories, and dedicated stock/asset portfolios (ticker symbols, units owned, average acquisition cost PRU, market prices, P&L updates, and buy/sell trade logs).
* **Cash Flow Intelligence & General Account KPIs:** Locally computed operational metrics for the General Account (all-time and period record expenses/incomes, top expenditure and revenue tags, starting/ending balances with absolute and percentage variations, and daily spending averages).
* **Evolution & Trend Curve Snapshots:** Locally computed 00:00 daily, weekly, and monthly vault snapshots (for 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y, and All-Time trend curves) with automatic First-In-First-Out (FIFO) local retention pruning, real-time dynamic P&L % touch scrubbing, and period change breakdowns (separating net capital inflows from net investment P&L).
* **Cash Flow, Calendar & Planned Commitments:** Everyday income/expenses, interactive monthly calendar items, future planned transactions with scheduled due dates, and cash flow inclusion settings ("Include in the cash flow" toggle).
* **Dividend Tracker & Projections:** Dividend records (gross amounts, net payouts, tax withholding percentages, payment frequencies, linked stock vaults, multi-horizon forecasts, and monthly average reference indicators).
* **Recurring Rules & Alerts:** Scheduled recurring cash flows, monthly due days, Day 10 trial end cancellation reminders, and device push notification schedules.
* **Tags & Categories:** Custom user-defined tags, color labels, and category allocation settings.
* **User Preferences & Custom Layouts:** Selected currency symbol (e.g., €, $, £, R$), language settings, persistent custom vault order (via Drag & Drop 3-dots handle), sort options (including Custom Order), reminder notification schedules, and view mode preferences (compact vs expanded, list vs calendar).
* **Temporary Pro Passes:** Local timestamp tracking expiration of ad-rewarded Pro Passes (30 seconds each, limited to 5 passes per 24-hour window). Upon pass expiration or subscription conclusion, all screens, custom filters, and display modes automatically revert to safe default settings.
* **Backups & Biometric Reset Safeguard:** Private JSON backup files (including vaults, transactions, recurring rules, dividends, and trend snapshots) generated solely when you explicitly choose to export your data. Initiating a complete Backup & Factory Reset requires local device biometric authentication (fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN) to prevent accidental data erasure.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Technical Data
The App integrates trusted industry-standard SDKs solely for optional subscriptions and ad-rewarded passes:
* **In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play Billing):** Processes anonymous app user IDs, transaction receipts, active subscription status, and purchase dates to unlock and restore Premium features. See [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
* **Rewarded Video Ads (Google AdMob):** When you voluntarily watch a video ad to unlock a 30-second Pro Pass (up to 5 per 24-hour window), Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers (GAID), device diagnostics, and ad interaction data to prevent fraud. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Device Permissions
* **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** Used only for optional, locally scheduled daily check-ins, recurring transaction due alerts, planned transaction reminders, dividend payout reminders, and the automated Day 10 trial end reminder alert.
* **USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT:** Used strictly for local device authentication before executing sensitive actions such as a complete Backup & Factory Reset. Biometric data is validated solely by your device's operating system security hardware and is never accessed, processed, or stored by the App.
* **INTERNET:** Required only for Google Play Billing validation and optional AdMob rewarded video playback.

### 5. Data Deletion & Portability
You retain complete ownership: you can export your data to encrypted JSON or wipe all stored financial data at any time via the App's **Data Center** (protected by biometric authentication before executing a Factory Reset) or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.
